---
name: Blog publishing policy
description: Imported blog posts should become public automatically, including the existing imported drafts.
---

Publish blog posts automatically when the approved-source importer saves them. The user also explicitly chose to publish the existing imported drafts after being told that the current set includes profile-filter page lookalikes.

**Why:** The owner wants imported blog content to go live without a separate publish action and confirmed that existing imports are included.

**How to apply:** Keep the admin ability to unpublish posts. Ensure future discovery excludes escort profile, directory, and filter pages before saving and auto-publishing them.
