---
name: Escort feed posting
description: User decision on social-style content and publishing timing for escort feed posts.
---

Escort-authored feed posts should support text, emojis, photos, and videos, and become public immediately after submission rather than waiting for pre-publication admin review.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose social-style posts with media and immediate publication.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing profile entries in the feed while adding real escort-authored posts. Keep server-side ownership checks and media validation; immediate publication does not mean accepting spoofed authorship or unsupported files.
